### 👋 What is this?

A simple, honest log of what I'm learning as I work toward becoming a data analyst. No polish, no pressure to sound impressive, just daily notes on what I practiced, what clicked, and what still confuses me.

I started this because I noticed I'd learn something one week and forget I ever learned it a month later. This is my way of actually tracking progress instead of just *feeling* like I'm doing stuff.

---

### 🗂️ How it's organized

Each month gets its own file inside `/logs`. Entries are short — usually just 2-3 lines on what I worked on that day.
